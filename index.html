I am a postdoctoral researcher at CESQ and Université de Strasbourg, working in the group of Prof. Johannes Schachenmayer. I received my PhD from USTC in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Wei Yi.

My work focuses on theoretical problems in quantum simulation with cold-atom and open systems. I am particularly interested in the structures and general principles underlying quantum many-body phenomena.
