I am a Ph.D. student at Université de Strasbourg under the supervision of Arthur-César Le Bras and Juan Esteban Rodríguez Camargo. My research interests lie in arithmetic geometry and the Langlands program, especially the p-adic aspects.

Before that, I was a normalien at École Normale Supérieure de Paris, and at Université Paris-Saclay. I obtained my B.Sc. in 2022 at Tsinghua University.
